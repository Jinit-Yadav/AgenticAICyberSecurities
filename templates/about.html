About CyberShield AI

Next-generation cybersecurity powered by advanced machine learning

Our Mission

CyberShield AI was developed as a major project to demonstrate the power of ensemble machine learning in cybersecurity threat detection. Our mission is to create an intelligent, adaptive, and accessible security system that can protect infrastructure from evolving cyber threats.

Key Achievements

  • 87% threat detection rate with ensemble learning
  • Real-time monitoring of 40+ network features
  • Multi-expert AI analysis with debate system
  • Support for UNSW-NB15 and CIC-IDS2017 datasets
  • Automated response and IP blocking capabilities
