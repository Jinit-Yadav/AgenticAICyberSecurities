Frequently Asked Questions

Is CyberShield AI free to use?
Yes! This is a major project demonstration. All features are freely available for testing and evaluation.
What datasets are used for training?
Our models are trained on UNSW-NB15 and CIC-IDS2017 datasets, along with real attack logs.
Can I integrate this with my existing system?
Yes! We provide RESTful APIs for programmatic integration with your security infrastructure.
How accurate is the threat detection?
Our ensemble model achieves 82.4% accuracy with 87% threat detection rate using XGBoost + LightGBM.
